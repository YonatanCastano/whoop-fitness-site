ABOUT WHOOP FITNESS

Fitness data with a clear purpose.

WHOOP Fitness is built around a simple idea: people should be able to understand their activity, recovery, and progress without getting lost in complicated numbers.

We designed this concept to make performance information easier to use in real life—at the gym, during recovery, and between goals.

OUR FOCUS Help people make informed choices every day.

HOW IT STARTED

More than numbers on a screen.

The team noticed that fitness trackers can collect plenty of information, but users may still be unsure what it means for their next workout, their sleep, or their recovery.

WHOOP Fitness was created as a way to connect those details to useful next steps, turning information into a better daily routine.

CLARITY Present useful insights without unnecessary complexity.
BALANCE Recognize rest and recovery as part of long-term progress.
CONSISTENCY Support habits that people can build and maintain over time.
